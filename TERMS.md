# Terms of Service

**Myshcat's Services (Discord bot)** — last updated 2026-09-26. This is a plain-language
summary of how the service actually works, not a substitute for formal legal advice.

## What this is

A Discord bot providing a middleman escrow service, a build/farm order service, and a
support ticket system for a DonutSMP Minecraft community. By using its commands or services
you agree to these terms and to Discord's own [Terms of Service](https://discord.com/terms).

## Middleman service

- A 10% fee applies to mediated trades; minimum trade size and staff rank limits are shown
  in the bot (`/trade limits`).
- Staff act as good-faith mediators, not guarantors. We make reasonable efforts to prevent
  scams (staff-only claiming, rank-based value limits, three-way completion confirmation) but
  cannot promise a specific outcome to every dispute.
- A trade only completes when all three parties (both traders and the assigned staff member)
  confirm it in the ticket.

## Build/farm service

- Orders queue only after payment is confirmed — the service does not extend credit or
  build first.
- Refund policy: 75% back if an order is cancelled while still queued; 50% back if a
  delivered base is raided within 48 hours of delivery (staff-approved claim). These
  percentages may be adjusted by server ownership and are shown in the bot at the time of
  order.
- Builders are paid their listed cut 48 hours after delivery, subject to strikes/void
  conditions described in-server.

## Support tickets

Tickets are handled by server staff on a best-effort basis. Misuse (spam tickets, abuse of
staff) may result in ticket or server access being restricted.

## Acceptable use

Don't use the bot to defraud other users, circumvent staff decisions, or violate Discord's
Terms of Service or Community Guidelines. Server staff may deny service, cancel a pending
(unpaid/unconfirmed) trade or order, or remove access for abuse.

## Availability

The service is provided "as is," with no uptime guarantee. Payment auto-verification
depends on a third-party game API; if it's unavailable, the bot falls back to manual staff
verification.
